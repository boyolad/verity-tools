Tips for an accurate photo
  • Shoot side-on. Stand level with the wall's edge so you see its profile, and hold the phone upright.
  • Vertical references: a hanging rope, a plumb line, a door frame, a gym support post.
  • Level references: a calm horizon or a level floor edge. Avoid tree trunks and rock edges.
  • No reference? Use Live sight. Its photos carry the phone's own gravity reading.

Measure by contact

Press the back of the phone flat against the wall, screen facing you. Any rotation works.

Tips for contact readings
  • Holds in the way? Lay a board or straightedge across the face and press the phone to that.
  • Lock freezes the reading so you can step back and read it.
  • Check it first: on a door or wall you know is vertical it should read close to 0°.

Measure with the camera

Stand side-on to the wall. The blue line shows true vertical. Capture a frame, then trace the wall.

Safari will ask for motion and camera access. Tap Allow both times.
